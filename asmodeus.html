34 / Philosophy

The manifesto

Fourteen principles that guide my work. I wrote them in one sitting, on a plane, and have never revisited them, which I consider proof of their correctness.

One. Ship it. Whatever it is. Whatever state it is in.

Two. A problem you have not been told about is not a problem you have.

Three. Documentation is a confession. Write none.

Four. The person who names the meeting controls the meeting.

Five. Never be the last person to touch a file before it breaks. Be the second to last.

Six. Speed is a substitute for correctness in every organisation I have worked in.

Seven. If you cannot fix it, rename it. A "known issue" requires no action. A "bug" does.

Eight. Every estimate should be a number you would be comfortable tripling in public.

Nine. Complexity is job security. Simplicity is generosity. Choose one.

Ten. The postmortem is not about the outage. The postmortem is about the postmortem.

Eleven. Do not conquer a village that has a druid. Conquer the other 99%. Declare victory. Leave.

Twelve. Reply to no email and you will be thought thoughtful. Reply to every email and you will be thought unemployed.

Thirteen. Take credit early, before the outcome is known. Credit taken before the result is credit that cannot be revoked.

Fourteen. None of this is advice. All of it is what I actually do. Those are different things and the difference is the entire point of a manifesto.

I have been told these principles are "concerning". I have been told this by people who report to me, which suggests they are working.

Post-incident review: INC-0412

Published in the spirit of blameless postmortems. Blame has been assigned separately, in a different document, to a specific person, by name.

Summary

On a Friday at 17:02, a deploy was pushed directly to production with checks disabled. Between 17:02 and 17:14, the checkout service returned HTTP 500 to 100% of requests. The second window was worse and was not detected for eleven hours.

Impact

Approximately 41,000 orders were accepted and destroyed. Customers received confirmation emails. The confirmation emails were the only artifact of the transaction that survived. Several customers have framed them.

Detection

The incident was detected by a customer on social media. Our monitoring did not fire because the alert had been muted in 2021 by an engineer who no longer works here, whose name I will not repeat, but which I have repeated in four other documents.

The root cause was a Friday afternoon deploy with checks skipped. The deeper root cause was that the checks had been failing for six weeks and had been skipped every time, which is a process issue rather than a personal one, and I want to be clear that I am the process.

51 / Colophon

Included because every self-respecting personal site has a colophon, and because it is another section, and sections are the point.

Typography. UnifrakturMaguntia for the wordmark, Cinzel for headings, Familjen Grotesk for body text, JetBrains Mono for data. Comic Sans appears in fourteen places and every one of them is deliberate.

Growing up, my mother never had a phone number. In those days you simply knew where someone lived, and you went there, and if they were not home you waited in the garden until they were. The garden had a particular smell in late summer. I want to talk about that smell for four paragraphs before I give you an email address.

My grandmother came from a small village. Not that village. A different one. She used to say that a man who gives out his contact details too quickly is a man with nothing to hide, and that a man with nothing to hide has not lived. I think about this every time somebody asks for my email, which is often, and which I do not answer, in her memory.

The first time I heard from a recruiter
